Central Pattana, The Mall Group and Siam Piwat agreed to SpoonAsia featuring their projects with official images (confirmed by Tor, Nov 2026). These are hotlinked from each owner's own site/CDN (centralpattana.co.th, media.themallgroup.com, siampiwat.com's CloudFront), listed per project in `owner-photos.json`, shown first in each album and credited "Photo courtesy of <owner>" with a link to the source page. CenTRal cENtrAL uses the official renderings from Central Group's newsroom (CPN / Mitsubishi Estate announcement, 8 Jul 2026). Silom Complex has no official image on any Central Pattana page, and silomcomplex.net now redirects to an unrelated gambling site, so it is not linked. Projects run by Central Retail / Central Group (Central Chidlom, Central Embassy, Robinson Bangrak) are not covered by the Central Pattana agreement and keep their open-licence photos.
