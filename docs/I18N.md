# Japanese edition (i18n)

English is the default and lives at the root (`/analysis`). Japanese lives under `/ja` (`/ja/analysis`).

## How it works
- `src/i18n/ui.ts` holds the UI strings (nav, footer) for `en` and `ja`, and the **`translatedRoutes`** list.
- `src/i18n/utils.ts` holds the helpers: language detection, `localizedHref`, and `getAlternates` (used for hreflang tags and the switcher).
- `BaseLayout.astro` sets `<html lang>`, the canonical URL, hreflang + `x-default`, `og:locale`, and loads Noto Sans/Serif JP on `/ja` pages only.
- `@astrojs/sitemap` writes `sitemap-index.xml` with hreflang pairs. `public/robots.txt` points crawlers to it.

## Shipping a Japanese page
1. Create the page under `src/pages/ja/` with the same path as the English one, e.g. `src/pages/ja/analysis/index.astro`.
2. Add its English route to `translatedRoutes` in `src/i18n/ui.ts`, e.g. `'/analysis'`.
3. Done: nav and footer links, the language switcher, hreflang and the sitemap all pick it up.

A route that isn't in `translatedRoutes` gets no hreflang, and Japanese readers are linked to the English page. They never hit a 404.

## Rules
- Untranslated content shown on a `/ja` page carries the `英語版のみ` (English only) badge and `lang="en"`.
- Japanese copy must be written or reviewed by a native business-Japanese editor before publishing. Never ship raw machine translation.
- Internal links have no trailing slash (`trailingSlash: 'never'`).
